- **Goals:** 146.367 predicted versus 122 actual (+24.367, +20.0%). MAE changed from 1.431 to 1.420. 26/42 team estimates were high and 16/42 were low.
- **SOG:** 1478.060 predicted versus 1207 actual (+271.060, +22.5%). MAE changed from 7.523 to 7.041. 35/42 team estimates were high and 7/42 were low.
- **SAT:** 2201.440 predicted versus 2479 actual (-277.560, -11.2%). MAE changed from 9.336 to 9.860. 9/42 team estimates were high and 33/42 were low.
- **Model opportunity is unchanged.** Predicted xSAT and TOI are retained. Historical personal bucket percentages and their average fallbacks are unchanged. Offensive volume is scaled to 90% at the matchup stage and the matching defensive contribution rises to 20%; eligible keys are unchanged. This is not evidence of independently validated accuracy.
- **Winner record: 12–9.** Always picking the home team would have produced 10/21 correct selections. Several predicted margins remain very small.
- **The additive attempt formula remains experimental.** Offense and defense describe overlapping matchup opportunities; a close total SAT does not prove the added attempts have the right distribution. Changes here come from the matchup weights, not player TOI or baseline offensive rates.
