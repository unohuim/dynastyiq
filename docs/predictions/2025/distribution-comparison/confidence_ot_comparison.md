**OT columns:** actual OT includes games ending in overtime or a shootout. “Actual spread <2” counts OT candidates whose official final score difference was less than two goals, including the shootout standings goal. Both OT hit% and <2 spread % use the number of OT candidates in that row as the denominator. These are separate success measures and may overlap.

**Totals:** SAT, SOG and goals include all games in the confidence group, including OT candidates, and are displayed as whole numbers. Goals exclude shootout standings goals. Total percentages are recalculated from pooled counts, not averaged across groups. An empty completed group has 0–0 and N/A percentages; a pending run uses dashes throughout.
